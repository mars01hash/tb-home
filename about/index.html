Your Software Engineering Partner

TechBeez designs, builds and runs web, mobile, AI and cloud products for startups and growing businesses, with design, engineering and product expertise under one roof.

For more than six years we have helped founders and product teams turn ideas into software that people use every day.

We work across fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, SaaS, logistics, education and ready-made garments. In the garment industry we go further: we build and run TB-ERP, our own AI-based ERP for exporters, which reads tech packs and POs and carries every order from costing to closing.

Whatever the industry, the approach is the same: a small senior team, a working build in the first week, a release every week, and code that belongs to you from the first commit.

From First Conversation to Final Launch

Five stages, one team, and a working build in front of you every week.

  1. Discovery

    We turn your goals, users and constraints into a written scope and a cost range before you sign anything.

  2. Design

    We map the journeys that matter and test a clickable prototype with real users.

  3. Development

    Reviewed code, automated tests and a working build within seven days, then a release every week.

  4. Launch

    Store submissions, cloud setup, monitoring and a launch checklist, handled with you.

  5. Growth

    We keep improving the product on a support plan, or hand it over with documentation and runbooks.
