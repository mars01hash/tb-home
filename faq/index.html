General

What does TechBeez do?

TechBeez is a software development company. We design, build and run web, mobile, AI and cloud products for startups and growing businesses, with design, engineering and product expertise under one roof.

Which services do you offer?

Mobile app development, web and SaaS development, AI solutions and automation, product design, MVPs for startups, cloud and DevOps, legacy modernisation, QA, dedicated teams and TB-ERP, our ERP for garment exporters. See all services.

Which industries do you build for?

Mainly RMG and apparel manufacturing, fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, SaaS, logistics and education. Over 6+ years we have delivered 100+ projects for 50+ clients across these markets. See our work.

Do you work with clients outside your country?

Yes. Most of our work is remote, with overlapping hours agreed at kickoff, a shared channel for daily questions and a weekly video demo.

Projects and pricing

How does a project with TechBeez work?

Discovery, design, development, launch and growth. You see a working build within seven days of kickoff, then a release, demo and changelog every week. See our process.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so we give you a written estimate with a range after a free scoping call. You know the cost of the first release before you commit to anything.

Fixed scope or dedicated team: which should I choose?

Fixed scope suits clear requirements and a fixed budget. A dedicated team suits a live product with a roadmap that changes every week. Many clients start fixed and then switch. Read the full guide.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks of agreeing the scope. Your first working build lands within seven days of kickoff.

Can you take over a project another team started?

Yes. We start with a short code audit and give you a plain list of what to keep, fix and rewrite before any larger commitment.

AI

Can you add AI to my existing product?

Yes. We start with a short review of your product and data, pick one workflow where AI clearly saves time, and ship it behind a measurable goal before expanding.

Which AI models do you use?

We choose per use case, mostly from OpenAI, Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini models, and keep the model behind a separate service so it can be changed later without touching the rest of your product.

Is my data used to train AI models?

We use business API tiers whose terms do not use your data to train the provider's models, and we only send the data a feature needs. We agree the setup with you before any data leaves your systems.

RMG and TB-ERP

What is TB-ERP?

TB-ERP is our AI-based ERP for garment exporters. AI reads buyer tech packs and purchase orders, then merchandising, costing, TNA, sourcing and order closing run in one system. Read the case study.

Which modules does TB-ERP include?
  • AI tech pack and PO reader with cross-checking
  • CAD fabric consumption and IE costing imports
  • Bulk and average costing, budgets and approvals
  • TNA calendars, daily tracker and sample requisitions
  • Supplier POs, sales contracts and order closing reports
How accurate is the AI tech pack reader?

AI cross-checking in TB-ERP reaches 99% accuracy, and every flagged mismatch goes to a merchandiser before costing. Users report around 80% less time on costing, bulk orders, tracking and management.

Can TB-ERP replace our spreadsheets gradually?

Yes. We clean your master data first, then go live module by module in the order work flows, starting with merchandising. See our migration checklist.

DPP and traceability

What is the EU Digital Product Passport?

A structured digital record of a product's materials, origin and care, linked to the product through a data carrier such as a QR code. It comes from the EU Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation, with textile rules expected in the coming years. Read our guide.

Can you help us prepare traceability data for buyers?

Yes. We help exporters link materials, suppliers and certificates to each order and style, so buyer and DPP requests become a report rather than a project.

Ownership and security

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first commit. We work in your repository and your cloud accounts, and the contract assigns all intellectual property to you.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We are happy to sign your NDA, or ours, before the scoping call.

How do you keep our data secure?

Least-privilege access, separate environments, encrypted secrets, code review on every change and audit logs where the product needs them. Access is removed at the end of the engagement.

After launch

What happens after launch?

Either we keep running and improving the product on a support plan, or we hand it to your team with documentation, runbooks and paired sessions.

Do you offer support and maintenance plans?

Yes. Plans cover monitoring, security updates, bug fixes and a monthly budget for improvements, with agreed response times.
