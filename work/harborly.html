Harborly · Fintech

A savings and payments app, from first sketch to both app stores in 8 weeks

A first-time founder came with an idea and a deadline. We designed, built and launched a regulated savings app on iOS and Android.

The challenge

Harborly's founder had a clear idea, savings goals with round-ups and instant transfers between friends, but no technical team and a launch date tied to a partner bank. The app had to feel simple while handling identity checks, payments and a full audit trail.

What we built

  • One Flutter codebase for iOS and Android, with biometric sign-in and push notifications.
  • Onboarding with identity checks through the partner bank's KYC provider.
  • Savings goals, round-ups and transfers, with every money movement written to an append-only ledger.
  • An admin web app for support and compliance teams to review accounts and transactions.

How we worked

A clickable prototype was tested with twelve target users in the first two weeks. From then on, the founder got a working build, a demo and a changelog every Friday, so every product decision was made with the latest version in hand.
