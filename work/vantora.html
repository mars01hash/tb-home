The challenge

Vantora's clinics booked appointments by phone into a legacy system that only two people dared to change. No-shows were high, there was no way to see a doctor remotely, and every new clinic made the system slower.

What we built

  • A patient portal for booking, rescheduling, forms and secure messages.
  • Video consultations built into the clinician's day view, with notes saved to the patient record.
  • Automated reminders by SMS and email, with one-tap confirm or reschedule.
  • A gradual migration that moved one clinic at a time from the legacy system, with data checked at every step.

How we worked

We started with a two-week audit of the legacy system and wrote tests around its riskiest parts before changing anything. Privacy was designed in: role-based access, audit logs and encryption at rest and in transit.
