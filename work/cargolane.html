Driver and dispatch apps that keep working offline, run by the same team for three years

The challenge

Cargolane's old driver app needed a connection for every step. In rural areas and basements, drivers could not scan parcels or capture proof of delivery, so failed deliveries and support calls kept rising.

What we built

  • An offline-first driver app that stores routes, scans and signatures locally and syncs when a signal returns.
  • Route optimisation and live ETAs shared with customers by SMS.
  • A dispatch dashboard showing every vehicle, stop and exception in real time.

How we worked

We rode along with drivers before designing anything. Since launch, the same engineers have shipped improvements every two weeks under a support plan.
