Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information TechBeez collects through this website, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Details you send us, such as your name, work email, company and project description when you use the contact form.
  • Technical information your browser sends when you visit, such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed, which our hosting provider may log.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project.
  • To run, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the website and handle email, under agreements that require them to protect it, or where the law requires it. Enquiries sent through our contact form are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, which processes them under its own privacy policy.

Third-party content

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, icons from jsDelivr and images from Unsplash. When your browser requests these files, those providers receive your IP address under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and for up to two years afterwards unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we work together under a contract.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its use. To make a request, email info@techbeez.tech.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
