How We Hire

Four steps, clear feedback at each one, and a decision within a week of your team interview.

  1. Apply

    Send your CV or portfolio and a few lines about work you are proud of.

  2. Intro call

    30 minutes with the hiring lead about you, the role and the team.

  3. Practical task

    A short practical task, or a walk-through of your own past work.

  4. Team interview

    Meet the people you would work with, then a decision within a week.
