FAQ

Questions from first calls

Have you built something like my product before?

Very likely. Over 6+ years we have delivered 100+ projects for 50+ clients across web, mobile, AI and cloud. On the scoping call we walk you through the past work closest to yours.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so we give you a written estimate with a range after a free scoping call. You know the cost of the first release before you commit to anything.

How quickly can you start?

Scoping usually begins within a week of the first call. A build team is typically available two to three weeks after the estimate is agreed.

Can you add AI to my existing product?

Yes. We start with a short review of your product and data, pick one workflow where AI clearly saves time, and ship it behind a measurable goal before expanding.

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first commit. We work in your repository and your cloud accounts, and the contract assigns all intellectual property to you.

How do you keep quality high?

A second engineer reviews every change, automated tests run on every commit, and a failing build cannot be deployed. Performance and accessibility are checked on each release.

What happens after launch?

Either we keep running and improving the product on a support plan, or we hand it to your team with documentation, runbooks and paired sessions.
