How the AI Works

Merchandisers review and approve. The AI does the retyping and the checking.

  1. Upload

    Drop in the buyer's tech pack and purchase order: PDFs, scans or phone photos.

  2. AI extracts

    Styles, BOM lines, colourways and size-colour quantities are read into structured data.

  3. AI cross-checks

    The PO is compared with the tech pack, style by style and size by size. Mismatches are flagged.

  4. Cost and plan

    Costing, TNA, material booking and supplier POs start from the same checked data.

Built to be trustedThe AI extracts only what is written in the documents, never guesses a missing value, and skips anything crossed out or marked cancelled. Every mismatch goes to a person before costing.

How We Roll It Out

No big-bang cutover. Your factory keeps shipping while TB-ERP goes live.

  1. Discovery

    We map your current files, buyers and approval flow, and agree which modules go live first.

  2. Master data

    Buyers, suppliers, fabrics, trims and styles are cleaned and de-duplicated before import.

  3. Module by module

    Merchandising and costing first, then planning, sourcing and closing, with a short parallel run each time.

  4. Training and support

    Role-based training on your own live orders, then ongoing support and improvements.
